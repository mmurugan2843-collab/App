Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install any complex software? +
No! ClientFlow runs directly in any modern web browser on your phone, tablet, or PC. You can also save it to your home screen as a web app.
Can I use ClientFlow without configuring Firebase right away? +
Yes! ClientFlow includes an instant offline/demo storage engine. You can test customer management, follow-ups, analytics, and CSV exports immediately, then connect Firebase whenever you want cloud persistence.
Can other businesses see my client details? +
Never. All customer data is strictly isolated to your authenticated account ID using rock-solid Firestore Security Rules.
Can I export my data if I decide to cancel? +
Absolutely. You can export your full client list and history to standard CSV at any time with one click.
